The business asked for a dashboard. I changed what we were building.

In 12 field shadows, not one farmer opened more than two data sources before deciding — they wanted a verdict, fast. A dashboard adds cognitive load to people already overloaded. The defensible bet was to remove choices, not add views — and I made that argument to leadership.
