05For designers

AI tools, shifts and news that matter for product, UX and UI work.

  • Scissor ↗ Free

    A free vector and pixel editor that runs in the browser with no account or upload; your files never leave your device.

  • MengTo/skills ↗ Free

    75 MIT-licensed agent skills for designers building UI with Claude, Codex or Cursor, 62 of them for web design, motion and 3D.

  • Claude Sonnet 5.5 ↗

    A faster, cheaper everyday model for prototyping and writing: 30%+ faster than Sonnet 5 at the same per-token price.

  • Gemini Skills (from 17 Nov) ↗

    Gems become Skills you call with a slash and stack in one chat, a shift from separate assistants to composable tools.

  • ElevenLabs v4 ↗

    A more expressive voice model; v4 Turbo runs at about 150 ms, fast enough to prototype real voice agents.

  • Momentic Mo ↗

    An AI QA engineer that walks through your app's flows without test scripts and files bugs with repro steps and video.

  • Manus 2.0 Studio ↗

    Adds professional editing tools, including a video editor, and event-triggered Automations to Manus's agent.

  • Cloudflare cf ↗

    An agent-first command-line tool for the whole Cloudflare API, handy if you ship your own prototypes on Cloudflare.

  • Study: AI summaries distort memory ↗

    Georgetown and UW found inaccurate AI summaries made people remember the original events worse. It's a design problem for every summary UI.

  • Supabase: 60% of new databases start from AI tools ↗

    A signal of who is building now: AI coding tools are widening the pool of people who ship real products, designers included.

One move today · under 10 minutes

Open Scissor in your browser and redraw one icon from your current project. You'll know in ten minutes whether a free, no-upload editor can cover your quick vector work.
