Siemens had the workforce method. The operating system around it was broken.

32% of jobs change radically by 2030 — and no one knew which. Siemens' #NextWork methodology answered that on paper. Over 11 months I led design on the multi-persona product that made it operable for 8 stakeholder archetypes across an HR ecosystem serving 300,000+ employees.

The temptation was to redesign the methodology. The job was to leave it intact.

For three months the team treated #NextWork as raw material — renaming phases, adding a sixth. The owners pushed back, rightly. The unlock came in week 11: the real risk wasn't a clumsy UI, it was software de-skilling the HRBPs trained to apply judgement. That reframed everything.
